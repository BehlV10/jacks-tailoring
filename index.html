A Lifetime Dedicated to the Art of Fit

When Jack Papazian opened his doors at 1937 Massachusetts Avenue in 1986, Porter Square was very different, but his philosophy has never wavered: true tailoring is an individual craft, not an assembly line.

With more than 40 years of hands-on tailoring experience, Jack has earned a revered reputation among Boston business executives, Harvard and MIT academics, style enthusiasts, and couples preparing for their wedding day.

“Unlike shops where an attendant pins your garment and hands it to a back room or an outside factory, I cut and sew every piece myself. You see the person doing the work, and I stand behind every stitch.”

Known for his direct and honest advice, Jack will tell you frankly what can and cannot be done with a silhouette. He treats your wardrobe with the same care and discernment as his own.

In-Person Craftsmanship

Walk-in Fittings & In-Person Estimates

Jack does not provide quotes over email or text message. True tailoring requires assessing fabric drape, seam allowance, and anatomical posture on your body in person.

Preparing for Your Fitting

To ensure an exacting fit on your first visit, Jack recommends bringing the following:

Proper Shoes / Heels

Bring the dress shoes, boots, or heels you intend to wear. Trouser breaks and gown hems depend directly on exact heel height.

Collared Shirts & Foundation Wear

For suit jackets, wear a proper dress shirt. For bridal gowns, bring the specific foundation garments or bra cups you will wear on the event day.

Standard Turnarounds

Standard alterations typically take 7–10 days. For urgent deadlines or weddings, call Jack directly to confirm rush accommodation.

No appointment required. Simply walk into the shop with your garments during open hours.
