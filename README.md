# Jack's Tailoring & Dry Cleaning — Modern Redesign

A high-end, responsive single-page website for **Jack's Tailoring & Dry Cleaning**, a Cambridge landmark established in 1986 at 1937 Massachusetts Avenue (Porter Square).

🔗 **Live Website on GitHub Pages:** [https://behlv10.github.io/jacks-tailoring/](https://behlv10.github.io/jacks-tailoring/)

---

## About the Shop
* **Proprietor:** Master Tailor Jack Papazian (40+ years experience)
* **Location:** 1937 Massachusetts Ave, Cambridge, MA 02140
* **Phone:** (617) 868-8339
* **Specialties:** Bespoke Suiting, Wedding & Formal Alterations, Leather Jacket Relining & Repair, Precision Denim & Trouser Tailoring, Eco-Friendly Dry Cleaning.
* **Core Philosophy:** *"The man who measures you is the man who does the work."* All measuring, cutting, and sewing is done on-site by Jack himself.

---

## Upgrade Highlights
* **Mobile-First & Responsive:** Replaced the legacy 2006 fixed 575px `<table>` layout with a modern, fluid design.
* **Sartorial Luxury Aesthetic:** Deep obsidian navy palette, warm gold accents, and Cormorant Garamond typography.
* **Crawlable Local SEO:** Semantic HTML5 and schema.org `LocalBusiness` / `DryCleaningOrLaundry` JSON-LD data.
* **Curated Social Proof:** Real verified reviews and citations from Styleforum, Reddit `/r/boston`, Yelp, and Google.
* **Conversion Tools:** Sticky mobile call & directions bar, dynamic open/closed badge based on operating hours, and an interactive fitting inquiry form.
